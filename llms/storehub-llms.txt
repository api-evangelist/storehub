# StoreHub

> StoreHub is Southeast Asia's leading all-in-one cloud-based point-of-sale (POS) and business management platform. Founded in 2013 and headquartered in Malaysia, StoreHub powers over 18,000 restaurants, retailers, and service-based businesses across Malaysia, Philippines, and Thailand. With a 4.7-star rating from 2,200+ Google reviews, StoreHub provides everything businesses need to operate, grow, and succeed—from their first store to their first chain.

## Company Overview

StoreHub Sdn Bhd (Company No: 1072290-D) was founded in 2013 with a mission to make technology accessible for everyone, empowering all businesses to grow sustainably and successfully. The company believes small businesses are the backbone of communities and deserve access to enterprise-grade technology that was previously only available to large corporations.


## Why StoreHub Exists

In today's fast-changing world, businesses must embrace technology not just to survive, but to thrive. However, the best technology has traditionally been limited to large corporations with big budgets. StoreHub believes this is wrong. Small businesses—the cafes, restaurants, boutiques, and service providers that make up our communities—deserve the same powerful tools.

StoreHub was built to solve this. It provides enterprise-grade business management technology at prices small businesses can afford, with simplicity that anyone can use.
